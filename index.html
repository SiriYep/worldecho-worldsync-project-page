WorldSync strengthens action following at three complementary levels: the action distribution, the video representation, and the response to controlled action interventions.

Abstract

Action following needs evidence beyond expert replay.

Action-conditioned world models are increasingly used as learned simulators for policy evaluation and improvement, but their utility depends on whether generated futures reflect the numerical actions supplied to the model. We introduce WorldEcho, an evaluation protocol that pairs five action-query categories with reference RoboTwin rollouts and evaluates both visual integrity and SE(3) end-effector trajectory alignment.

Qualitative analysis identifies two off-expert failure modes: visual collapse and visually plausible but action-inconsistent motion. We also present WorldSync, a training recipe that expands action coverage with simulated and target-domain real expert trajectories, grounds intermediate video features with an auxiliary Action-Forcing Expert, and supervises the effect of changing actions through paired interventions. In the current 50-task RoboTwin evaluation, WorldSync obtains a task-macro integrity-gated error of 0.0661 and an 84.51% visual pass rate. These results are preliminary and remain subject to revision.
